How we work.

Good gardens get better with age. We build them to do that, with sound materials, careful planting and the upkeep to see them through.

The same gardener every visit
Your maintenance visits are always with the same person, so they get to know your garden.
Trained horticulturists
Everyone on the team holds an RHS Level 2 qualification or higher.
A year’s guarantee on planting
If anything we plant fails in its first year, we replace it free of charge.
Wildlife-friendly as standard
We use peat-free compost, avoid pesticides and include plants for pollinators in every design.
